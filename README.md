A make.fun bounty entry: the funniest one-line farewell a phone says as its battery hits 1 percent.

## The entry

> I kept your passwords, your photos, your 2 a.m. confessions — and after reading your search history, I'm relieved to be dying.

The phone speaks in its own voice, about its own dying battery — and the punch lands at the end: after a lifetime of keeping the owner's secrets, it has seen the search history, and dying suddenly sounds like the good option.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22.18+) validates the line against the gate — 1–199 UTF-8 characters, a single non-blank line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
